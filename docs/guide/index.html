01

Welcome

Wealth 360 is a servicing desk for a wealth bank. Agents do the routine work, Salesforce keeps the rules and the record, and a named person makes every decision that matters.

Who this guide is for Relationship managers who work cases on the Wealth 360 desk, and presenters who demonstrate it. It explains what to click and what happens next, in plain terms. The technical design, the evidence behind every claim and the path to production are in the Wealth 360 Solution Handbook. Every screenshot comes from the sandbox or the walkthrough recordings of 29 October 2026, on fictional data; email addresses and the names of build team members are masked.

1.1What Wealth 360 does

A servicing team spends most of its day on a few requests that keep coming back: a client moves house, a client wants money out, a payment arrives that nobody can match, a family loses someone, a client complains. Each request needs the same checks across the client record, the KYC file, the payee and the sanctions list, and each must leave a trail a reviewer can follow. Most of the effort goes into gathering and checking. Very little of it is the decision itself.

Wealth 360 takes the gathering and checking off your desk. When a request arrives, two agents work it step by step and cite their evidence. When something needs judgement, it comes to you with the evidence and a proposed action. You read it, decide, and confirm by name.

1.2Who does what

Case agent

The maker. Reads the request and its documents, finds the client, runs the checks, drafts letters and proposes the next step. Cites a quote and its source for every step.

Review agent

The checker. Reviews the case in a fresh context against the rules, without seeing the Case agent's reasoning. Sends work back when it is not right.

You

The relationship manager. Decides every outcome that needs a person: release money, clear a check, send a letter, book a meeting, or send the case back.

Salesforce

Holds the journeys, rules, records and audit trail. Decides who may do what. Refuses any decision by an agent, whatever the agent is told.

The one rule to rememberAn agent can never stage, approve or release a decision. Salesforce refuses it. Every decision in Wealth 360 is recorded against the named person who confirmed it.

1.4What is live and what is not

Live in the sandbox Live

  • The desk in Claude, reading and writing Salesforce as you
  • Both agents, working cases on their own within seconds of a change
  • Outside checks: UK Sanctions List, payee account check, identity check, address lookup
  • Email into a case, and branded email out on the bank's letterhead
  • The Salesforce apps: case cockpit, client page with KYC tab, Admin app

Demo only Not real

  • All clients, accounts, documents and amounts are fictional
  • Every demo client's email points to one test inbox
  • Releasing a payment records the authorisation in Salesforce; no money moves
  • Payee and identity checks run against provider sandboxes; the payee check is not Pay.UK Confirmation of Payee
  • Meetings are recorded in Salesforce; no calendar invitation is sent
  • Client emails so far reached Salesforce through the deployed handler as simulated emails; the first real inbound email is still to be proven

04

Making a decision

Every decision runs in three steps: stage, read the plan, confirm. Nothing changes in Salesforce until you confirm.

4.1Stage, read, confirm

  1. Read why the case is with you. The box at the top of the drawer gives the reason in one sentence. Check the proposal on Overview and the evidence on Checks and Activity.
  2. Choose. Select the main button at the foot of the drawer. Its wording depends on the case (table below).
  3. Read the plan. Salesforce stages the decision and shows a Plan to confirm: every step it will take, for example record the decision on the case, release an instruction, confirm a meeting, log the decision in the audit trail. A warning line says where any email will go. A short code under the plan identifies this exact plan.
  4. Confirm or cancel. The confirm bar repeats the decision in words. Select Confirm to carry it out, or Cancel to change nothing.
  5. See the result. The plan turns into a Result with each step done, and the drawer says "You decided. The agents are carrying it out." A short notice at the bottom of the desk says the decision was saved to Salesforce.

Behind Confirm is a single-use token tied to the plan and to you. A changed plan, a second use of the same token or another person is refused. The decision is recorded against your name, never against an agent.

4.2The decision buttons

ButtonWhere you see itWhat it does

You can also decide from chat, for example "Approve the bereavement letter and book Tuesday at 10". Claude stages the same plan, shows it to you word for word and waits for your confirmation.

4.3Sending a case back

When the agents got something wrong or missed something, send the case back rather than fixing it yourself. Write what they should do differently. The note is staged like any other decision, as Investigate, so it shows in the plan and in the audit trail.

4.4What happens after you decide

  • The case moves to Agents working. Your decision wakes the agents within seconds.
  • The Case agent carries out the steps that follow: it records the release, sends the letter you approved, confirms the meeting, records anything still outstanding.
  • The last step of the journey closes the case in Salesforce. The case then shows in Completed with the tag After your decision, and counts towards the Completed figure in the header.
  • A rejected decision never reaches fulfilment.
  • If an email fails to send, the decision stands and the failure is logged on the case for a person to follow up.

5.2Sanctions match on an incoming payment

RemitterVictor BondarevBest matchHenry LockwoodCase00001365JourneyMoney in, raised to L3LaneDecide

£22,000 arrived by Faster Payment from Victor Bondarev with the reference "LOCKWOOD H 31447720". A money in case with no client yet is named after the remitter, so the card reads Victor Bondarev. 5.3Change of address, straight through

ClientPriya RamanCase00001373JourneyChange of details, L1LaneCompleted, By agents alone

Priya Raman emailed to say she had moved to 2 Barons Court Road, London, and attached a water bill. No person touched this case.

  1. The email became a case and the card appeared in Agents working within seconds.
  2. The Case agent read the email, found Priya in Salesforce and read the bill. It compared the name and the new address with the record and checked the address with the postcode service.
  3. The Review agent checked the case independently.
  4. Because the case was a clean level 1 change, Salesforce let the agent carry it out: it updated Priya's address, sent her an address change confirmation and closed the case. A case closed this way shows in Completed with the tag By agents alone and counts in the header's "by agents alone" figure. In the sandbox this took about two and a half minutes from the agent's first step to the closed case.

When the agents may finish on their own

Salesforce checks every one of these. If any fails, nothing changes and the case comes to you with the reason.

  • A change of address at L1, with no exception, the evidence read and compared, and the independent review passed.
  • The address check found the new address; it has a valid UK postcode and differs from the record.
  • The client's identity is verified on file.
  • No open money out case for the client, and no other address change in the last 30 days.

In Salesforce Priya's mailing address is the new one, the old address is kept on the case timeline, and a copy of the confirmation email sits on the case. Other attempts show the fences at work: on case 00001372 another open change of details case existed, so the agent handed it to a person.

Point outEvery fence was checked in Salesforce code, not in a prompt. Anything unusual still goes to a person.

ClientHenry LockwoodCase00001371DocumentCouncil tax bill (fictional)

Sometimes the evidence arrives on paper or as a photo. Claude can read it on the page.

5.8Everyday actions

ClientEleanor WhitfieldCase00001362

Around every case, the everyday actions are one click away in the action menu. Each one is a standard Salesforce task or event, on the client and on the case.

  1. Log a call. Choose the direction (for example "I called the client"), the minutes and what was said. Select Log call. It is saved as a completed call in Salesforce and on the case timeline.

06

The Salesforce side

Everything on the desk lives in Salesforce. The same cases, clients and rules are in two Lightning apps: Wealth 360 for relationship managers and Wealth 360 Admin for the people who run the agents.

6.1The case cockpit

Every case is a standard Case record in Financial Services Cloud with the Wealth 360 cockpit on the page. Open it from the desk with the arrow button in the drawer, or from Cases in the Wealth 360 app.

  • The header with the journey, level, owning team and service level, and "Agents last ran HH:MM, next HH:MM".
  • A seven-stage track: Intake, Plan, Execute, Verify, Draft, Check, Fulfil.
  • Tabs for the decision, the evidence, documents and activity, one row per agent step, each attributed to the identity that wrote it.
  • How a closed case ended, in the field W360_Resolution__c: Resolved by agents, Closed after decision or Superseded. The desk's Completed counter and column read this field.
  • The customer request, why it needs you, the checks with their sources, a note field, and the proposal with its buttons. Deciding here works the same way: a confirm dialog before anything changes.

6.2The client page and KYC tab

The client page carries the Wealth Servicing tab: cases that need you, unmatched payments that may belong to the household, items waiting on others, and KYC for each household member. The KYC tab shows the verdict and next review date, every identity verification with its session, every sanctions screening, the identity documents, and the outside checks recorded on the client's cases (figure 5.10).

07

Email

Email is the channel clients actually use, so it is a full loop: a client's email becomes a case, the bank's emails carry the case reference, and the client's reply finds its case.

7.1How a client's email reaches a case

The bank's intake address is a Salesforce email service. Each email is filed by the first rule that fits.

#RuleWhat happens
1A reply to a bank emailThe subject carries the case reference in square brackets, for example [W360-00001380], or the footer carries the thread reference W360REF-.... The email and its attachments are filed on that case.
2A known clientThe sender's address matches one client's email: a new case for that client. When several clients share the address, the client named in the subject or body is chosen.
3Unknown or unclear senderA case for a person, level 3, never worked by the agents.
4Sender checkAn email that does not pass the sender authentication checks is raised to level 3 with the reason.
5JourneyKeyword rules set the journey when exactly one fits (for example "passed away" for bereavement). Otherwise the Case agent classifies it as its first step.

Replies. A reply restarts the agents only when the case is Waiting on client. If the case is with a person, it keeps its place and the reply waits on the case for that person. A reply from a different address is filed but raises the case to level 3. Matching a sender identifies a client; it does not prove who sent the email, so a request from email that needs a decision always ends with a person.

7.2What goes out, and when

Every bank email goes out on the Wealth Servicing letterhead, from the Wealth Servicing address, with the case reference in the subject and footer. A copy is kept as a file on the case. Types: document request, address change confirmation, bereavement letter, payment confirmation, complaint response, decision letter and general.

Sent byWhen
Your releaseWhen you confirm a decision that releases a letter or email. It goes to the client's email on record. The plan says where before you confirm.
Your Request documentsAt once, to the client's email on record, optionally with an identity check link.
The agents, on their ownOnly two kinds, and only when Salesforce's fences hold: a document request (switched on in Agent control, to the client's email on record, on an allowed domain, fewer than three a day per case, and the only link it may carry is an identity check), and an address change confirmation at the end of a straight-through change. Everything else waits for a person's release.

7.3Testing by email

In the proof, every demo client's email address points to one test inbox. Ask the build team for access to it and for the intake address. The cases in this guide reached Salesforce as simulated emails through the deployed handler; a real email over the internet, and delivery into Accenture mailboxes, are still to be proven. Test it before a demo, never for the first time during one.

  1. Start a new case. From the test inbox, write to the intake address. Name the client in the subject or body (for example "Priya Raman: I have moved"), because the inbox belongs to every demo client. Attach the document if the request needs one.
  2. Watch the desk. The case appears in Agents working when the agents start.
  3. Reply to a case. Reply to the bank's email, which arrives in the same inbox, so the subject keeps the reference, for example [W360-00001380]. If the case is waiting on the client, the agents restart.

A good demo shows one idea: agents do the work, people decide, Salesforce enforces it. This chapter gives a 10 to 15 minute script, a checklist and what to say about safety.

8.1Before the demo

  • Both connectors connected, named exactly Wealth 360 and Wealth 360 Checks. Sign in again if a token has expired.
  • The plugin at version 0.12.0 or later. Open the desk once and confirm the status line reads Live · Salesforce.
  • In Wealth 360 Admin › Agent control: agents On, the journeys you will show switched on, working hours off or covering your slot, spend today well under the daily cap.
  • The cases you plan to show are in the lane you expect. Decided cases do not go back by themselves (section 8.3).
  • A Salesforce tab open on the Wealth 360 app, signed in, for the Salesforce side.
  • For a live email: access to the test inbox, and one test send done earlier the same day.

8.3Resetting and seeding

  • Decisions stick. A case you authorise, accept or approve moves on and closes. It does not return to your lanes. To show the same moment twice, ask the build team to seed fresh cases, or log a new request live.
  • Seeding is a build team task. The demo clients, accounts, payees and cases are created by scripts in Salesforce, and a clean-up script removes them. Do not run them yourself. Ask a day ahead.
  • Fresh live cases are the most convincing: a new case by phone (5.4) or an email from the test inbox (7.3). Allow two to five minutes for the agents to finish.
  • Timing. The agents start within seconds of a change. A full run on a case takes from about one minute (a hand-over) to about five minutes (a complaint with one rework). Do something else on screen while they work.

8.4What to say about safety

The core lineAn agent can never stage, approve or release a decision. Salesforce refuses it, whatever the agent is told.
  • Separation of duties. A Case agent makes, a Review agent checks in a fresh context, and a named person decides.
  • Enforced in Salesforce, not promised by a prompt. Agents may raise a level, never lower it. Steps out of order or without evidence are refused.
  • Two identities. You sign in as yourself. The agents sign in as a dedicated agent identity. Every step is recorded against whoever took it.
  • Stage, show, confirm. Every decision is shown as a plan first, and runs behind a single-use confirmation tied to you.
  • Email is fenced. Agents send only document requests and address confirmations, to the client's own address on an allowed domain. Everything else waits for a person.
  • Off switch. Pause agents in the Admin app stops them from the next minute.

09

Troubleshooting and FAQ

Most problems come from a connector: its name, its sign-in, or the Salesforce user behind it.

9.1Troubleshooting

The desk does not load data, or says it cannot reach Salesforce.

Check the connector is named exactly Wealth 360, with the space, and is connected. Reconnect it and sign in to Salesforce again. Then ask Claude to open the desk in a new conversation.

The Checks tab says the connector is missing.

Add or reconnect Wealth 360 Checks (section 2.3). The rest of the desk works without it.

I cannot see the Wealth 360 app or the Admin app in Salesforce.

Your user needs the W360_Relationship_Manager permission set, and for the Admin app W360_Agent_Admin. Ask the build team.

Confirm was refused.

The plan changed, or it was already used, or another person staged it. Close the drawer, open the case again and stage the decision afresh. Your user must also hold the right to decide.

A new case does not appear under Agents working.

Check Agent control: agents On, the journey switched on, working hours, and spend today under the cap. If the agents are paused, the run log shows Skipped runs. A sweep every two minutes catches anything missed.

A case has sat in Agents working for a long time.

Open Activity to see the last step. If nothing has moved for ten minutes, ask the build team to check the run log. Do not decide around it.

The identity check result has not come back.

The client may not have finished. The result comes back within a few minutes of the client finishing; select Get result on the Checks tab to ask at once.

A plugin update does not appear in Cowork.

Remove the marketplace, add cowork-offering/wealth-360 again and reinstall the plugin.

Analyse a document does nothing.

Claude asks once for your OK to read documents on the page. Accept it. Use a PDF or a photo.

9.2Frequently asked questions

Can an agent move money or send a letter on its own?

No. An agent prepares; a person releases. The only emails an agent may send on its own are a document request and an address change confirmation, fenced by Salesforce.

Can I change what the agents do?

The rules are custom metadata in Salesforce, shown in the Rulebook. The business changes them through a metadata deploy; the agents follow the change on their next run. Agent control switches a journey on or off from the next minute.

Does the Review agent see what the Case agent was thinking?

No. It checks the case in a fresh context, against the rules and the original request.

Who is recorded when I decide?

You, by name, on the case, the staged plan and the audit trail. Agent steps are recorded against the agent identity.

What does Claude see?

On the desk, Claude reads Salesforce through your connector, as you, and a document only when you choose to analyse it. The agents read the case through their own identity.

What happens to the client's documents?

Emails and attachments are stored as files on the case in Salesforce. In the proof they are fictional.

Can I use real client data?

No. Fictional personas only, in Salesforce and at every outside provider.
